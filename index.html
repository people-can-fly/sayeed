Research

Measuring and managing cyber risk

Organizations need risk assessments they can repeat, compare and act on. AssessITS sets out step-by-step procedures and practical evaluation metrics for IT and cybersecurity risk assessment. riskAIchain pairs AI-driven IT infrastructure with a blockchain-backed approach to make risk management more reliable.

Related: AssessITS, riskAIchain

Threats to finance and national security

cryptoRAN reviews cryptojacking and ransomware attacks on the banking industry: the threats, the challenges and the open problems. ISC2 asked for permission to include its findings in a cybersecurity white paper. ISNDchain looks at how blockchain can secure the information systems used in national defense.

Related: cryptoRAN, ISNDchain

Data quality for trustworthy analytics

Security and business analytics are only as good as the data behind them. dataLDA is a systematic review that compares traditional ways of analyzing data quality in big data with latent Dirichlet allocation, a topic-modeling method.

Related: dataLDA

AI-enabled digital twins for ports

A digital twin is a live virtual model of a physical operation. This work studies how AI-enabled digital twins can support sustainable, carbon-neutral smart port operations, starting from a comprehensive review of digital twins for smart seaports.

Related: WMU Journal of Maritime Affairs, 2026, DSI 2025

IT leadership and digital performance

My sole-authored HICSS 2026 paper examines how the presence of a chief information officer (CIO) relates to employees’ digital performance inside organizations.

Related: HICSS 2026

Where this work is going

I am extending this risk-assessment work toward AI-supported threat detection: models that help security teams spot emerging attacks earlier, reduce false alarms and decide which incidents to handle first, in sectors such as banking, healthcare, cloud services and critical infrastructure.

Interests: cybersecurity and privacy, data analytics and data mining, machine learning and neural networks, simulation, IT and business alignment, IT-enabled business agility, database management, network security and performance, optimization.
